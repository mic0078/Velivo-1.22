# ❓ Guide – Videos and TV in Velivo

*Working version. Eventually in Velivo under the F1 key and in the player's ⚙ menu.*

---

## 1. First steps

The top Velivo bar has four buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🎬 | Opens the **video library** – all your videos with descriptions and covers. |
| ▶ | Opens an **empty player**. The browser hides to the tray and comes back when you close the player. |
| 📺 | Turns on **TV** – straight away the last channel you watched; the browser hides to the tray. Open the channel list with the 📺 icon on the player bar or by right-clicking 📺. |
| 🧹 | **Clean-up and backup** – cleaning and backing up your data. |

When nothing is playing, the player shows a black screen with the Velivo logo.

**Resuming a video.** If you stopped a video, Velivo asks whether to resume it the next time. You can change this in Settings → Video player: *ask*, *always resume* or *always from the start*.

---

## 2. The player bar

The bar appears when you move the mouse and hides after a moment.

**On the left – playback:**
- ⏮ ⏸ ⏹ ⏭ – previous, pause/play, stop, next.
- −10 s / +10 s – seeking, 1× – speed.

**In the middle:**
- ☆ **Favourites** – adds a channel to Favourites or a video to the "⭐ Favourites" playlist. A gold star = already a favourite.
- 🌙 **Sleep timer** – stops after 15–120 minutes, at the end of the video or (for TV) at the end of the programme. You can tick "💻 Then shut down the computer" – you have 60 seconds to cancel. In the last 30 seconds the sound fades gently.
- ⧉ **Picture in picture (PiP)** – a second channel or video in a small window.
- CC subtitles, narrator, audio track, aspect ratio, frame snapshot, library, always on top, mini mode, full screen, volume.
- Double-clicking a video file in Windows opens just the Velivo player straight away – the browser doesn't pop up (it waits minimised on the taskbar).
- When Velivo starts with Windows, it quietly prepares TV (channels, TV guide) and the player – after opening everything works at once.

**Progress bar:**
- for a video – time and seeking,
- for live TV – instead of the bar: **the station name in bold │ a scrolling line with what's on** (title and a short description), with the programme times and **● LIVE** at the sides (live TV can't be seeked),
- for catch-up – the time within the programme and **⏪ CATCH-UP**.

**For live TV**, instead of −10 s / +10 s / 1× there are buttons to go back through channels (Velivo remembers the last 20 you switched to – also after closing TV and restarting; going back doesn't erase the history):
- **↶ Previous channel** – each click goes back one channel, in order,
- **⇤ Back to the first** – straight back to the channel you started switching from,
- **🕘 Channel history** – a list of recent channels (alphabetical), a click switches.

**Quick icons on the right:**

| Icon | Action |
|---|---|
| ⏺ | Record this channel (red when the channel you are watching is being recorded) |
| ⏹ | Stop recording – works from any channel; with several recordings it shows a list |
| ⏱ | Recording margin – how many minutes before and after the programme |
| 📁 | Recordings folder |
| 📺 | TV channels |
| 📅 | Full TV guide |
| ℹ | Info bar |
| 🔄 | Reload the channel |
| 🎞 | Playlists and my queue |
| ⭐📺 | My favourite channels (alphabetical) |
| ⭐🎬 | My favourite videos (alphabetical) |
| 👁 | Control bar: after how many seconds it hides (or always visible) and how transparent it is |
| ⚟ (sliders) | Info bar: showing on mouse movement, hiding time, transparency, text size |

TV recordings are only in the "📺 Recorded from TV" library – they don't go into "All videos", because they have no descriptions or posters.

At the very end of the bar (after a separator):
- a **network graph** of the last 30 s – the blue ↓ line is downloading (TV, videos from another computer), the green ↑ line is uploading (a video for another computer when watching together over LAN), with both speeds,
- the **time and date**,
- above them the **stream quality**: e.g. "FHD 1920×1080 · 6.8 Mb/s · 50 fps"; an orange "⚠ dropped: N" = lost frames (the computer is stuttering), a drop in Mb/s = the provider's stream is stuttering. For a video the video codec is added (e.g. H.264), and hovering shows the file's full technical details.

Above them:
- **⏺ REC** – a red pill while something is recording (channel and end time). Click to stop or extend it.
- **🔌 Provider connections** (on one line with "LIVE") – how many connections you are using now (watching, recordings, PiP). Click and set how many your provider allows – a warning appears only when you exceed it.

While something records, a small red dot shows at the top of the picture: **"Recording: channel name"** (also with the bar hidden and while watching another channel).

**Keyboard shortcuts:**

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space | pause / play |
| ← → | 10 s back / forward |
| ↑ ↓ | volume |
| F, double-click | full screen |
| M | mute |
| I | info bar |
| L | channel / video list |
| PgUp / PgDn | previous / next channel |
| F5 | reload the channel |
| G / H | subtitle shift |
| [ ] | speed |
| P / N | previous / next video |

## 3. Right-click menu

Right-click the picture. The menu contains only what isn't an icon on the bar:
- **TV:** what's on now and next, **⏮ From the start of the programme** (catch-up), **⏩ Live**.
- **📂 Open file…**
- **🕘 Recently watched TV** and **🕘 Recently watched videos** – the last 20 each, newest at the top. A channel started from here plays in its own group.
- **📋 Channel / video list**, **⏰ Schedule playback**, subtitles and narrator. Set the on-picture clock by clicking the clock with the date on the bar.
- **⚙ Settings** – list transparency, connection options, channel buffer, recording margin. The control bar (👁) and info bar (slider icon) settings are at the end of the quick icons.

Things that are icons on the bar (pause, favourites, recording, PiP, sleep timer etc.) are not repeated in this menu – that keeps it short.

---

## 4. The list on the left

Open it with **L** or move the cursor to the left edge of the picture.

**For TV:**
- at the top you choose a group (Queue, Favourites, Recently watched or a group from the list) – Velivo remembers it,
- each channel shows what's on, the times and the programme's progress bar,
- ★ favourite, a **green dot** = the channel has catch-up, ⏺ = the channel is recording.

**Right-click a channel:** open, favourite, record in the background, show in PiP, ✏ edit channel, the channel's TV guide, sorting.

**For videos:** groups (queue, recently watched, all, playlists), right-click – open or show in PiP.

---

## 5. Video library

- Columns: title, year, rating, genre, length, watched, last watched, added. Click a header to sort – Velivo remembers the sorting and column widths.
- Select several videos by dragging with the mouse and choose from the right-click menu: play as a queue, add to the queue, add to a playlist.
- **🎲 Pick random videos** – when you don't know what to watch.
- **✏ Rename**, **🔎 Look up information online**, **📝 Fill in information manually**.
- Descriptions and covers can come from Jellyfin and Filmweb.

---

## 6. TV

- In the **📺 TV** window you add channel lists (M3U), choose groups, search channels and see the TV guide.
- The TV guide loads in the background – also when you start a channel from the player without opening the TV window.
- **✏ Edit channel** (right-click): your own readable name and icon (from a file or an address). "Restore original" goes back to the name from the list. The TV guide keeps working.
- **Channel buffer** (click the network graph on the bar – the connection options are there too): 1 s – the smallest delay; 3–5 s – when the picture stutters.

---

## 7. TV catch-up – watching what already aired

If your provider offers catch-up (e.g. 3 days back), the channel has a **green dot**.

- In the channel's guide and in the full TV guide, past programmes are **green with ⏪**. **Double-click** – you watch from catch-up.
- **⏮ From the start of the programme** – when a programme is already on and you want it from the beginning.
- From catch-up you watch like a video: pause, seeking.
- After the programme ends, the next one plays from catch-up, and then Velivo returns to live. **⏩ Live** – straight to live.

Velivo picks your provider's catch-up format by itself and remembers which one works.

---

## 8. Recording

- **⏺ Record now** – records to the end of the current programme (plus the margin).
- **From the TV guide** – right-click a programme → Record this programme.
- **⏱ Recording margin** – how many minutes before the start and after the end (programmes like to run over). By default 2 and 10 minutes.
- **2 minutes before the end** Velivo asks: keep recording? (+15 / +30 / +60 minutes or finish on time).
- **You can watch another channel** while one is recording – these are two separate connections to the provider.
- If you close and restart Velivo during a scheduled recording, the recording resumes.
- Recordings are in the 📁 recordings folder and in the video library.

---

## 9. Picture in picture (PiP)

A second channel or video in a small window – with TV and with a video.

- Turn it on with the ⧉ icon, from the menu or with "Show in PiP" in the channel list, the TV window or the library.
- Choice: TV (recently watched, favourites, this group, all groups) or Videos (recently watched, favourites, all, open file).
- **Click the preview** – swap it with the main picture (a video remembers its position).
- **Drag** – move the window; **drag the frame** – resize it. Velivo remembers the position and size.
- The preview bar: **🔈/🔊** – sound from the preview or from the main window; for TV ⏮ ⏭ change channel, and after a separator a scrolling line with what's on and the programme's end time; for a video ⏸ ⏹ and seeking; ✕ close.

---

## 10. Sleep timer and scheduling

- **🌙 Sleep timer** – see chapter 2.
- **⏰ Schedule playback** – choose a video or channel and a time, and Velivo starts it by itself.

---

## 11. Subtitles and narrator

- **💬 Download subtitles…** – from the right-click menu (on the video or in the library). Subtitles come from OpenSubtitles.com: you sign in once with your own account (the password is stored encrypted on this computer), Velivo searches by file hash and by name, and saves the chosen subtitles next to the video as `film.pl.srt` – they load automatically.
- **G / H** – shift subtitles when they are late or early; Velivo remembers it for the video.
- **Narrator** reads subtitles aloud; the narrator settings are remembered for each video.

---

## 12. 🧹 Clean-up and backup

**Cleaning junk** – you tick what to delete (page cache, thumbnails, channel icons, old TV guide, logs). Each item shows its size. Passwords, bookmarks, channel lists and the library stay.

**Backup:**
- **Basic** – just your data (~90 MB).
- **Full** – everything except junk, including the TV guide, icons and narrator voices (~0.5 GB). After restoring nothing needs to be downloaded.
- Manually – "💾 Incremental backup now" (only changes since the last backup; if there is no full backup of that kind yet, a full one is made) or "💾 Full backup now" (a fresh complete one, e.g. before a big change or to move to another drive) – or automatically: daily or weekly. The folder is best on another drive or a USB stick.
- **Incremental backups** (on by default): a full backup every 7 days, and only changes in between – usually a few MB. When restoring you point to any backup, and Velivo assembles the full one with the increments. The last 5 full backups are kept together with their increments. Keep backups from one chain in one folder.

**After a serious failure:**
1. From the backup file, unpack the **_Instalator** folder and run **Velivo-instalator.exe**.
2. In Velivo click **🧹 → ♻ Restore from backup…** and point to the backup file.
3. Velivo restarts – everything is as it was.

Passwords are encrypted for your Windows account – restore on the same account.

- When a menu is open, clicking the picture only closes it – the video and TV keep playing.

## 13. When something doesn't work

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The TV picture stutters | click the network graph → Channel buffer 3–5 s; fewer connections at once; an HD version instead of FHD |
| No sound | check the 🔈/🔊 icon in PiP and the 🎵 audio track |
| Catch-up doesn't start | try again – Velivo tries the next formats; send vlc.log |
| Connection warning | click 🔌 and set your provider's limit |
| The file dialog is too big | Velivo adjusts it by itself; manually: Alt+Space → Restore |

**Files for reporting problems** (data folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Przegladarka`):
- `vlc.log` – the last video or channel (before sending, replace the token with XXX),
- `bledy.log` – program errors (appears only when something goes wrong).
