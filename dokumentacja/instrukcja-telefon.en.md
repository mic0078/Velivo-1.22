# Velivo for phones – manual

Velivo for Android is a light, private browser in Velivo colours with built-in TV, a film library, a video and music player (VLC) and a link to Velivo for Windows. It needs Android 10 or newer. No accounts, no adverts, no tracking – everything stays on the phone. The computer is the "main" device: anything coming from the phone is always confirmed on the computer.

> **👓 Good for people with weak eyesight – why:**
> - **everything zooms with two fingers** – menu, buttons, lists and page text (up to 220%), and each page remembers its own size;
> - **large tiles with icons and labels**, three to a row (two when zoomed a lot), large player buttons;
> - **clear colours** – dark, light or a **black-and-white high-contrast theme** (Theme in Settings);
> - **reader view** (just the text, large letters) and **read aloud**, also with the screen off;
> - **brightness and volume gestures** in the player, no hunting for small sliders;
> - **sticky notes with reminders** (doctor, medicines) and **protection against fake websites** – a red warning with one "Go back (safely)" button;
> - **no adverts or pop-ups** covering the content.
>
> Step-by-step setup: chapter 7.

This manual describes the full version (`Velivo-pelna.apk`). The Google Play versions differ only in YouTube – chapter 10.

## 1. Screen and menu

- **Address bar** – type an address or search words (searches go through Startpage).
- **🕶** – new private tab. **The button with a number** – the tab list with thumbnails (tap = switch, ✕ or a sideways swipe = close).
- **Velivo logo** – the menu. Tiles are in five groups, three to a row: **Tabs**, **This page**, **Films and TV**, **Mine**, **Settings**. At the top, the green "🛡 Blocked ads" counter – tap it for the privacy panel (chapter 6).
- **Swipe down** closes the menu and list windows (TV, library, notes, guide grid) and shows the page underneath – when the list is scrolled to the very top. Back closes them too.
- **Bottom bar** – ◀ back, ▶ forward, ⟳ reload, ⌂ start page, ＋ new tab (Quick Access).
- **The red "▶ YouTube" bar at the very bottom** – YouTube without adverts; press and hold: sleep timer and wake-up (chapter 8).
- **Press and hold on an advert** or an empty "ADVERT" box – Velivo asks whether to remove it.

- **Home-screen widget:** press and hold an empty spot on the home screen → **Widgets** → **Velivo**. It has **🔎 Search** (opens Velivo with the keyboard), **▶ YouTube** and **🗒 Notes** with the nearest dates ("today", "tomorrow", a date). It shows only your notes on the phone and refreshes after every change. It can be resized.

## 1a. Zoom (for weaker eyesight)

- **Spread two fingers** in the menu or the lists of tabs, bookmarks, history, passwords and notes – everything gets bigger; pinch – smaller (80–220%). The top bar and the bottom buttons grow too, and at large sizes the menu shows tiles two to a row.
- **On a page** two fingers change that page's text size (50–300%) – **every site remembers its own size** (the start page / Quick Access always stays at 100%), and Velivo remembers its own separately. Set it once.
- In **reader view** two fingers change the text size (up to 48 pt), like A− / A+.

### Reader view

- **A− / A+** – text size (remembered).
- **🔊** – read aloud from the reader; **✕** or Back closes it (and stops reading).
- Velivo **remembers where you left off** on each page and takes you back there next time. This stays on the phone only.

### Read aloud

- Carries on with the **screen off** and in other apps – the notification shows "🔊 page title" with a **Stop** button. It disappears once the article is finished.
- Going back (◀ or Back) stops reading.
- No speech engine or voice for the phone's language – Velivo offers to **download Google Speech Services**.
- On Xiaomi phones set: Settings → Apps → Velivo → Battery saver → **No restrictions**, otherwise the system may cut reading short.

### Removing elements with your finger

1. Menu → **🎯 Remove element**. The page gets a light violet overlay and a bar appears at the top.
2. Tap the advert – Velivo picks the whole advert box (violet outline) and asks **"Remove?"**: **Remove**, **Select a larger area** or **Cancel**. It also works on adverts inside frames (iframes).
3. On the bar: **⬆** – larger area, **📋** – copy the element's code (to report an advert Velivo doesn't hide).
4. **🗑** – remove. The element disappears and takes up no space; it stays hidden on this site on later visits too.
5. **↺** – restore everything removed on this site. **✕** – finish.

Quicker: **press and hold** on an advert or an empty "ADVERT" box – Velivo asks whether to remove it (on text and links a long press works as usual). Velivo checks every layer under your finger, so it also finds adverts hidden beneath a transparent layer of the page.

The rules are kept on the phone only (they do not sync with the computer).

### Sticky notes

1. Menu → **📝 New note** → choose a kind: 📝 Note, ✅ To do, 🛒 Shopping, 📞 Ring, ❗ Important, 💡 Idea, ⭐ For today.
2. Type. **The deadline** is a date in the text (e.g. `24.10`, `24.10.2026`, `2026-10-24`) – or tap **📅** and pick a day from the calendar (it is inserted at the cursor).
3. **🔁 Repeat deadline:** one-off, every week, every 2 weeks, every month, every year or **Custom interval…** (every N days, weeks, months, years). Velivo counts the next dates from the one in the text, e.g. "4.10 bins" every week → 11.10, 18.10…
4. **🎨** changes the colour, **🗑** deletes, **✓** (or Back) saves.

**Reminder:** a notification **the day before** ("⏰ Due tomorrow") and **on the day** ("⏰ Due today") – daily at about 9:00, also after the phone is switched back on. Tapping it opens the note. The **🗒 Sticky notes** list is sorted by the nearest date; the date and repeat show under the text.

## 5. Connecting to the computer

1. Phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi, Velivo open on both.
2. **The phone always invites:** menu → **Sync with computer → Connect to the computer (invite)**. For 3 minutes the phone sends an invitation.
3. The computer asks: "The phone … is asking to connect to Velivo on this computer. Connect?" → **Yes**. Compare the 6-digit code on both screens and confirm on both.

**Privacy:** the computer never looks for phones by itself – it only answers an invitation, and the phone sends one only for 3 minutes after you tap. Real-time sync (LAN) must be on on the computer. **Connecting a phone doesn't change the sync between your computers** – the computer keeps its key and the phone joins it.

With several computers use the **recovery file** (computer: Save recovery file…, phone: Load pairing recovery file).

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Bookmarks, passwords, Quick Access | From the computer to the phone – automatically while Velivo is open |
| New bookmarks and passwords from the phone | To the computer – the computer asks whether to add them |
| A file from the phone | **Share → Velivo** – pick a messenger contact (at home or over the internet) or the paired computer; the computer asks for consent |
| A file from the computer | On the computer 📎 Send a file to: phone; saved to Downloads |
| A tab from the phone | Menu → 📤 Send to computer |
| A tab from the computer | On the computer: right-click a tab → 📺 Send to… → phone; the phone opens it straight away (YouTube from the same point) |

## 6. Privacy

- **Privacy panel:** menu → tap the green "🛡 Blocked ads" counter at the top. It shows whether the connection is encrypted (🔒 https), how many adverts and trackers were blocked on this page and from which domains. **Turn off blocking here** – for a site that misbehaves with blocking (remembered for that site; **Turn on blocking here** undoes it).
- **Scam protection** (works on its own, offline, as in Velivo for Windows):
  - **fake sites** of banks and well-known companies (e.g. `mbank-logowanie.com`, `rnbank.pl`, `paypa1.com`, `olx.pl.dostawa-pay.xyz`, addresses with characters disguised as letters) – a red warning, **Go back (safe)** or **I know this site – continue**; fakes of sites you have saved passwords for are caught too;
  - **no "Get the app / Install / Add to Chrome" nags** – such boxes (e.g. on Startpage) are hidden, as in Velivo for Windows;
  - **app (APK) downloads from outside Google Play** – a warning, **Don't download** by default;
  - **HTTPS first** – pages open encrypted; if that fails, Velivo asks whether to open the page unencrypted (home network – router, NAS – without asking). A bad certificate is always blocked.
- The phone never sends its whole profile – only new items, which the computer confirms. The computer recognises the phone (shown as 📱 in the device list) and rejects packets from it that only computers send – the phone can't change the computer's settings.
- The connection is encrypted (AES-GCM) with the key from pairing; other phones on the network can read nothing.
- Your reading position, removed elements and settings stay on the phone.

- **Films and TV:** channel list addresses (often with a token) and Jellyfin sign-in data are encrypted on the phone (Android Keystore) and never go to logs or error messages. The channel list and guide are stored encrypted. Scheduled programmes remember only the channel name.
- **Videos and music on the phone:** Velivo reads them only when you ask (after Android's permission) and sends nothing.
- **🔐 Unlock with a security key (optional):** menu → Settings → 🔐 Security key. Adding and removing a key – after the phone password / PIN. Then passwords, banking mode, sync and the messenger open with a touch of the key (USB or NFC); cancel = fingerprint / PIN as before. Add a backup key; remove a broken or lost one individually (🗑). Every 3 months Velivo reminds you: **🧪 Check** – touch each key in turn and see which one works.
- **🔑 Security keys and passkeys** (YubiKey via USB or NFC, passkey): sites can ask for a key when you sign in (Android 14 and a recent WebView). Google may not offer them in apps outside its list of browsers – then sign in another way (e.g. "Try another way" → confirm on your phone).
- **No accounts, no telemetry:** Velivo sends no statistics or data to the author. A crash report is made only on the phone and goes anywhere only if you copy it yourself.

## 7. Velivo for people with weaker eyesight – step-by-step setup

Best set up once, together with the person who will use the phone:

1. **Size:** open the menu (the V logo) and spread two fingers in it until the text is comfortable (up to 220%). All of Velivo grows – the bar, buttons, menu, lists and page text. Velivo remembers it.
2. **Theme:** menu → 🌓 Theme → light or dark – whichever is easier to see.
3. **Reading:** on a long article, menu → 📖 **Reader view** (just the text, two fingers = larger text) or 🔊 **Read aloud** (works with the screen off too; Back stops it).
4. **Safety:** warnings about fake bank sites and about installing apps from outside Google Play work on their own – it is worth saying: "if Velivo shows **⚠ Warning**, tap **Go back (safe)**".
5. **Reminders:** 🗒 Sticky notes with a date (e.g. doctor 24.10) – a notification the day before and on the day; 🔁 for medicines every week or month.
6. **Adverts:** blocked straight away; for a leftover "ADVERT" box press and hold on it and tap **Remove**.

## 8. YouTube without adverts, picture-in-picture and music for sleep

> **First launch:** straight after installing (and once every few days, when Velivo refreshes its advert lists) the first page – e.g. YouTube – may take a few seconds longer to load, because Velivo is preparing ad blocking in the background. After that it is quick.

- **The red "▶ YouTube" bar at the very bottom** – opens YouTube (or returns to the open YouTube tab). Adverts are removed.
- **Plays in the background and with the screen off:** start a video or music, leave Velivo or turn the screen off – it keeps playing; **Stop** is in the notification.
- **The phone remembers where you stopped a video** (even without signing in to YouTube): when you come back to it, Velivo carries on from that point – "▶ Carrying on from 12:34". Watched to the end – next time from the start. Stored on the phone only (the last 300 videos), not in private or banking tabs.
- **Picture-in-picture (PiP):** when a video is playing and you go to the home screen, it moves to a small window above other apps (you can move it; tap to return to Velivo).
- **🌙 Music for sleep (sleep timer):** press and hold the red YouTube bar and choose **When this track ends** (YouTube won't start the next one) or 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 minutes – then the music stops by itself. With the screen off the notification has **🌙 Stop in 30 min** (each tap adds 30 minutes).
- **⏰ Wake-up / scheduled playback:** press and hold the red YouTube bar → **⏰ Wake-up with a track…** → choose the track: **from the current tab** (e.g. an open YouTube video), **from bookmarks** or **from history** → the time → when: **every day, Mon–Fri, weekend, chosen days** or **once**. At that time Velivo opens even on the lock screen and plays the track with the volume rising gently; **Turn off** or **Snooze 10 min**. In the same place: **▶ Try the wake-up now** and **Turn off wake-up**. The alarm icon in the phone's status bar shows a wake-up is set.
- The sleep timer also has **Custom time (minutes)…** – any number of minutes.

## 8a. Films and TV

### Player (VLC)

Velivo plays everything with the VLC library – the same as Velivo for Windows: TV channels (MPEG-TS, HLS), old AVI, MKV, MP4, AC3 sound, subtitles. Nothing to install.

- **Top bar:** title and what is on; **🗨** audio language and subtitles; **🌙** sleep timer; **⧉** picture-in-picture; **✕** close. On TV also **☆/⭐** favourite channel, **⏪** from the start of the programme (catch-up) or **⏩** live.
- **Middle:** ⏮ previous, ⏪ −10 s, ⏯, ⏩ +10 s, ⏭ next (on TV – previous / next channel in the list).
- **Bottom:** programme description (now, next, details), for films the progress bar and time.
- **Gestures (portrait and landscape):** left half up/down – **brightness**, right half – **volume** (☀ / 🔊 % indicator), sideways – seek in a film, double tap – ±10 s, tap – show / hide buttons.
- **Remembered position:** a film resumes where you stopped (Jellyfin – on the server too).
- **Picture-in-picture and background:** going Home – small window; with the screen off it keeps playing (notification with Stop). Closing the small window stops playback.
- **🌙 Sleep timer:** at the top **"📺 After this programme ends"** (TV – exactly when the programme ends, also after switching channels) or **"🎬 After this film ends"**, then 15–90 minutes. Playback stops and the phone goes back to normal standby.
- **Music:** audio files get a space-like VLC visualisation (if the phone supports it).

### 📺 TV

1. **Channel list:** ⚙ → add a list: M3U address (link from your provider) or a file on the phone, optionally a TV guide (EPG) address. Lists can also be sent from Velivo for Windows: right-click 🎬 or 📺 → **📱 Send Films & TV to phone** (large lists from files too). ⚙ → a list → **Edit / Delete**.
2. **Channels on screen:** A–Z by default (or favourites first / list order), search, **☰** group / ⭐ favourites / 📋 own lists. Under a channel: "Now … (until …) / Next …". **☆** – favourite. Press and hold a channel – 📅 its guide, add to an own list.
3. **📅 TV guide:** what is on now / today at 20:00 / tomorrow / at a chosen time; **⏰ Scheduled**; **📋 What's on** (channels of the chosen group A–Z → channel guide); **🗓 Guide grid** (channels A–Z × time, red "now" line, scroll sideways – forward and into the archive).
4. **A programme** (tap in the guide, grid, "what's on"):
   - **⏰ Turn on at 21:00** → "Turn on and keep playing" or "Turn on and stop when it ends". At that time Velivo turns the channel on by itself – also on the lock screen (system alarm).
   - **⏪ Watch from the archive** – past programmes (as many days back as the channel has, e.g. 3; at most 7) or the current one from its start. Velivo finds the catch-up format your provider uses (Flussonic, Xtream, shift, own pattern from the list) and remembers it. After a catch-up programme it plays the next one, and when it reaches "now" – live.
   - **▶ Watch now**, **ℹ Description**.
5. **Fast:** the list and guide are stored on the phone (encrypted) – TV opens at once, the guide refreshes in the background (every 12 hours), **⟳** forces a refresh. At night (charging + Wi-Fi + phone idle) Velivo refreshes lists, guide and library by itself.
6. **When a channel does not play:** after 25 s a message – many lists allow one connection at a time; stop TV on the other device (e.g. the computer).

- **📲 Films and TV without pairing (e.g. your mum's phone):** TV → ⚙ → **📲 Ask the computer for Films and TV** (or the button when adding a list). The computer (Velivo for Windows on the same network, LAN sync on) asks with a 6-digit code – the owner clicks **Yes**, the phone shows the same code and asks before saving. **Only** TV channel lists and the Jellyfin server address arrive (no key – sign in with your own Jellyfin user, e.g. "Mum"). **Passwords, bookmarks and Quick Access never go this way** – only after pairing. The exchange is encrypted with a one-time key. Note: the channel list gives access to your TV provider (often one stream at a time).

### 🎬 Library (Jellyfin)

- **No API keys:** Velivo finds the Jellyfin server on your home network → pick a user → password. The server gives the phone its own token (visible in Jellyfin → Devices, can be revoked). Or the settings come from the computer.
- **📂 API key from a file:** Jellyfin settings → **📂 Load API key from file (api.txt)…** – pick a .txt file with the key (32 characters 0–9, a–f). Addresses from the file: 🏠 home (e.g. http://192.168.8.117:8096) and 🌍 internet for away from home (e.g. http://mojdom.ddns.net:8097) – both at once; if missing – the current one or the server found on the home network.
- **Films and TV from the computer – choice window:** each TV list and the film library (Jellyfin) separately, with the address type: 🏠 home network, 🌍 internet, 💻 list from the computer (the address with a token is never shown). Only ticked items are saved. A request from the TV screen has Jellyfin unticked by default. The home and internet addresses of the same Jellyfin server are joined automatically.
- **Views:** continue watching, films, series (☰); sort A–Z / newest / year / 🕘 last watched / watched longest ago (only watched ones; a tap resumes where you stopped – also when watched on the computer). Series → episodes.
- The library opens at once from the phone's copy and refreshes from the server – films deleted on the server disappear, new ones appear.

### 🎞 My films and 📱 videos and music on the phone

- **🎞 My films** – your chosen list of files (＋ add, press and hold – remove from the list; the file stays on the phone).
- **📱 Videos and music on the phone** (Settings) – everything on the phone, split into videos, my music and built-in sounds; tap plays, ⏮ ⏭ move through the list.

### 🎵 My songs (YouTube)

Menu → **⭐ Add this song** on an open YouTube video (category, playlists) → **🎵 My songs**: A–Z / categories / newest, ☰ category or playlist, plays in order. Stored only on the phone.

## 8b. Velivo keyboard

The same keyboard as on the computer, in Velivo colours (dark, light or black-and-white theme – chosen in the keyboard app). Works in every app.

* **Layout like a regular phone keyboard:** 10 keys per row (wider, easier to hit). Extra characters – **long-press** a key with the small character above the letter (e.g. D → -, J → ;, M → /, digits → ! @ # …, comma → !, full stop → …); Polish letters as before (long-press A → ą). **Double-tap ⇧** – caps lock.
* **Separate app** “Velivo keyboard” (file `Velivo-klawiatura.apk`). **Turn on:** open the Velivo keyboard app → *Turn on* (Android settings), then *Choose*. Hold the space bar to switch keyboards.
* **Menu under the V logo:** tap the Velivo logo – instead of the keys you get 16 big labelled tiles: notes, search, YouTube, messenger, files, 🌍 translate, autocorrect, dictation, My dictionary, language, **♿ Accessibility** (Senior, Braille, Morse, sounds instead of words, read aloud, key sound, key height), theme, settings. Holding a tile says its name; tapping the logo again returns to the keys.
* **Swipe typing (flow):** slide your finger across the letters. Wrong word: ⌫ removes it, or pick another from the suggestion bar.
* **Polish letters:** hold a letter (a → ą) or turn on the **ą** key.
* **Autocorrect ✓**; ⌫ right after a correction restores what you typed.
* **Suggestion bar:** up to 6 words (slide the bar with a finger, “›” – there are more): completions, next word, ➕ adds a new word to My dictionary. Words you use often (also names and places outside the dictionary) and recently typed ones come first. When a word was uncertain, after space the bar shows other options – a tap replaces the previous word. The keyboard remembers a correction you picked (next time it fixes it by itself). A capitalised word mid-sentence (name) is not corrected.
* **💬 Phrases:** a sentence typed at least twice is offered on the bar (💬 …rest) – a tap inserts the rest. The first time only a fingerprint of the sentence is kept (no text), so one-off messages do not stay on the phone. Unused phrases disappear after a few months. Pasted and dictated sentences never become phrases. **💬 My phrases** (keyboard app): review, edit and delete phrases, add your own phrases and shortcuts (“adr = 5 Long St” – type adr and the address shows on the bar).
* **Learning from fixes:** when ⌫ takes you back into the previous word and you retype it correctly, the keyboard remembers your typical mistake and fixes it by itself next time.
* **⏱ Pace adaptation:** after about 30 letters the keyboard adapts the hold time (ą, symbols) to how long you usually press, and ⇧⇧ (caps lock) to your pace. Only taps on letters count (no dictation, paste, Braille, phrases or swipe) and only numbers are stored.
* **🔒 Learning and privacy** (keyboard app): learning from typing, phrases and pace adaptation – each can be turned off; “Learn only in Velivo apps”; **↺ Typing profile** reset. In password fields nothing is remembered or suggested; in incognito mode (when the app asks for it) suggestions work but nothing is remembered. Everything stays on the phone, encrypted – nothing goes to the internet.
* **🧹 Clear learned data** (keyboard app): deletes frequent words, pairs, remembered corrections and undone corrections – My dictionary stays.
* **📖 My dictionary:** one word per line – add or delete.
* **😀 Emoji** – the key next to space (an @ in e-mail fields): categories on top (🕘 recently used, smileys, feelings, gestures, hearts, animals, food, travel, things) and **GIF**.
* **🎞 GIFs from the phone** (Downloads and Pictures) – no third-party servers (Gboard searches Tenor by Google). First time: **🎞 Allow GIFs from the phone** in the keyboard app. The GIF goes to apps that accept them (WhatsApp, Messages, Velivo messenger…).
* **📋 Clipboard and editing** (button on top): recently copied texts (memory only, up to an hour, no passwords from password managers), arrows ◀ ▶, start ⇤ / end ⇥, Select all, Cut, Copy, Paste, Undo.
* **🔍 Big letter above the finger** – a big letter shows when you press a key (can be turned off in the keyboard app). Taller keys (↕) also mean bigger letters.
* **👵 Senior mode** (menu under V or the keyboard app): one switch for tall keys and big letters, the letter above the finger, words read aloud, no swipe typing and a longer press (a shaky finger does not make accidental words).
* **⠿ Braille typing** (menu under V → ⠿): the screen becomes 6 dots – left hand 1, 2, 3, right hand 4, 5, 6. Put fingers on the dots of a letter together and lift. One-finger swipe: right space, left delete, down new line, up read the text; two fingers down – back to the normal keyboard. Polish braille with ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż, digits after the number sign ⠼ (a–j = 1–0), capital ⠨, punctuation (EN: English braille). Characters go through normal typing – autocorrect, dictionary, suggestions and reading words 🔊 work.
  * **With TalkBack on** the system takes over touch – Velivo does not fight it and says to use the TalkBack braille keyboard or pause TalkBack.
  * **⠿ Learn braille** (keyboard app): *Explore* – the sign, letter and speech; *Exercise* – Velivo says a letter, you write it on the 6-dot field; “Learned: x of y”. Progress stays on the phone (can be turned off).
  * **⠿ Braille typing – settings:** speak characters, vibration, on-screen hints, phone screen facing away (columns swapped), save progress – a normal Android window (works with TalkBack).
* **😀 Emoji while typing:** for some words the bar suggests an emoji (love → ❤️, coffee → ☕; in English by emoji names) – a tap inserts it after the word.
* **🇬🇧/🇺🇸 English spelling** (keyboard app): British (colour, realise, centre) or American (color, realize, center) – when typing English the keyboard corrects to the chosen one (by rules; only when the other form is in the dictionary).
* **🌍 Translate** (menu under V → 🌍): the selected text (or everything typed) goes to the translator app you pick on the phone (e.g. Google Translate – works offline with downloaded languages); the translation replaces the text. Velivo has no translation server of its own and stores nothing.
* **Live hints:** in Braille – fingers on the dots and the letter shows on top at once; in Morse – after each dot / dash the letters matching that start show on top (with the rest of the code).
* **📖 Cheat sheet** (top right corner of the Braille and Morse field): all letters with Braille dots or Morse code; a tap returns to typing.
* **🤟 Fingerspelling keyboard** (menu under V → ♿ Accessibility → 🤟): hand drawings on letter keys (international alphabet), a small letter in the corner – typing with signs a deaf person knows, and learning the alphabet while typing. Tap 🤟 again for normal letters.
* **🤟 Learn fingerspelling** (keyboard app → ♿ Accessibility): *Explore* – sign and letter; *Practice* – a sign and 4 letters to pick, “Learned: x / 26”; *Word* – type a word to see it as a row of hands (short words fit the screen), ▶ letter by letter; **📷 Word sign** – a photo or GIF (with motion) of the whole-word sign, e.g. from a signing person – then shown when you type that word (your glossary). Progress stays on the phone.
* **·− Morse typing** (menu under V → ·−): left half of the screen – dot •, right half – dash —; the letter is typed after a short pause (longer in Senior mode). Short vibration – dot, long – dash. Polish letters, digits and symbols. Gestures as in Braille: right space, left delete, down new line, up read, two fingers down – normal keyboard. Speech, vibration and hints – in “⠿ Braille and ·− Morse – settings”.
* **🗣 Sounds instead of words** (menu under V → 🗣 or the keyboard app): for people who cannot speak clearly. Long “aaaa” (about 1 s) – confirm (Enter / Send), long “uuuu” or “eeuuu” – delete the last word. Best first *Teach “aaaa”* and *Teach “uuuu”* (3 s each) – the keyboard adapts to your voice. Normal talk near the phone does nothing (only a long steady sound counts). The microphone listens only while the keyboard is on screen; nothing is recorded or sent.
* **Cursor with a finger on the space bar:** slide your finger left or right on space – the cursor follows (easier to fix a letter inside a word).
* **🎤 Dictation:** the first time the keyboard app asks for microphone access. **🔊** reads aloud what you type. **PL / EN** – dictionary language.
* Nothing is learned in password fields. Learned words are encrypted on the phone.

## 8d. Velivo messenger

Chats, photos and files between your phones and computers with Velivo – no phone number and no third-party server. The Velivo browser or just the Velivo keyboard is enough; on a computer, Velivo for Windows.

* **Messages and calls always on screen:** when opened, Velivo checks whether the phone allows notifications, full-screen calls and running in the background (no battery saving) – if not, it asks you to turn them on (**Turn on** → settings).
* **🎤 Voice messages:** the 🎤 button in a chat – tap, speak, tap ⏹ to send (on Windows the same, by clicking). Tapping a voice message plays it in the chat (again – stop). They travel like files: at home, over the internet and via the 📬 mailbox.
* **💬 Velivo Messenger – a separate app:** `Velivo-komunikator.apk` works on its own (no browser or keyboard needed). When installed, on first start it takes over contacts and chats from the Velivo browser / keyboard (files from old chats stay in Downloads), and their 💬 button opens the Messenger. On the home network it uses port 41918 (Windows: firewall – a rule for TCP and UDP 41918).
* **📷 Camera photo:** the 📷 button in a chat (phone: camera app, Windows: webcam) – the photo is sent in full quality (original, not downscaled); on the phone a copy goes to the Gallery (Pictures/Velivo).
* **💾 Backup and a new phone / computer:** phone – 👤 → 💾 Backup (save dialog: Downloads, **Google Drive**, NUCBOX folder…), Windows – 🎨 → 💾 (any folder, also Google Drive). The backup holds contacts with keys, chats, identity and settings (no chat files), encrypted with your password – nobody can read it without the password, not even Google. On the new device: 📥 Restore from backup – contacts still know you. Phone backup → phone, Windows backup → Windows.
* **Chat – long-press in the list (Windows: right-click):** 📌 pin to top, 🔕 mute (8 h, a week, always), ⏳ disappearing messages (24 h, 7 days, 90 days – for both sides), 🛡 safety code (compare – the same = no one is impersonating).
* **🔍 Search in chat** (phone: 🔍 in the header, again – all; Windows: box at the top right), **✏ draft** stays with the chat, **📍 My location** (phone: 📎 → 📍, Windows: 📍) – a one-time map link, **⏩ voice messages** 1× / 1.5× / 2× and length (0:23); letter circles instead of dots.
* **🔗 My Velivo address** also on Windows: ➕ → 🔗 My address / I have someone's address (phone: 👤 → 🔗).
* **Messenger on Windows – convenience:** 🔢 unread count on the taskbar icon; no pop-ups or sound in full screen / presentation; shortcuts Ctrl+F (search), Ctrl+K (go to chat), Alt+↑/↓, Ctrl+Shift+M (mute), Esc; 🖼 photo and video viewer in Velivo (← →); 📎 → 📸 screenshot of an area; 😀 emoji; drag a file from the chat to the desktop; 🔝 chat in an always-on-top window and 💾 chat export (right-click a contact); ☑ Select… – several messages at once (forward, copy, delete for me).
* **📲 Phone update from the computer:** in Velivo on Windows right-click the contact → **📲 Send app update (APK)…** → pick the file from `build\android` (the folder is remembered). The phone chat shows “📲 New app version – tap to install” → tap → **Update** (Android always asks; the first time allow installing from Velivo). Contacts and chats stay. You can tap it yourself via 🖱 remote help.
* **📷 Photo and video with one button (like WhatsApp):** tap 📷 – full-quality photo, hold 📷 – record a video (up to 3 min), then preview and ➤ Send.
* **👁 Preview before sending (phone and Windows):** a photo, video, voice message, screenshot, pasted or chosen file is not sent right away – first a preview: ▶ Listen / 👁 Preview, then **➤ Send** or **Cancel** (a recording made in Velivo is then deleted). Forwarding (↪) – no second question.
* **🌍 Works on any network (Wi-Fi, mobile data, roaming, hotels):** when a network blocks the relay's usual port, the phone and laptop connect through port 443 (same as websites and WhatsApp). The relay (NUCBOX) opens it in the router itself; the 🌍 window shows “Backup port 443: open”. If “not open” – in the router forward TCP 443 to NUCBOX, to the relay port (e.g. 41921). Everything still goes only through your home, end-to-end encrypted.
* **Ticks and presence (phone and Windows, like WhatsApp):** ✓ sent (also a file waiting in the 📬 mailbox), ✓✓ grey – delivered (a mailbox file once the recipient’s device receives it), ✓✓ blue – read; the chat header shows “last seen …” (remembered after a restart too). Phone photos and videos are shown upright (portrait/landscape), also in the Windows viewer.
* **Messenger on the phone – new:** swipe ← → through full-screen photos and GIFs (long press – save to Gallery); long-press a message → ☑ Select several… (forward, copy, delete for me); contact menu → 💾 Export chat (TXT to Downloads); GIFs and stickers from the keyboard (Android 12+) go straight to the chat.
* **🖥 Remote help (in a video call):** on Windows the 🖥 button shows your screen instead of the camera (pick the entire screen). The other person (Windows or phone) taps 🖱 to ask for control; only after you agree can they use the mouse and keyboard (taps from a phone). A red “⏹ End control” bar stays at the top of the screen; ending the call ends control. Everything goes directly between you (at home or via your relay). Administrator (UAC) windows cannot be clicked remotely. **⇄ Swap sides** (while a screen is shared): after the other person agrees, the sharer stops and the other one starts sharing (Windows – click the highlighted 🖥, phone – Android asks). A computer without a camera or microphone can still call and share its screen (receive only).
* **📱 Phone screen in a call:** in a video call on the phone tap 🖥 → Android asks to start recording/sharing → the other person (e.g. on a computer) sees your screen, also when you switch to another app (the call stays audible). They tap 🖱 (Point at a spot) and click on your screen – a red 👆 circle appears for you for 3 s (needs the “Display over other apps” permission – Velivo asks once). Rotating the phone (auto-rotate) also rotates the picture for the other person. Stop: 🖥 again, “Stop” in the notification or end the call. 🖱 Phone control: the other person taps 🖱 (ask for control) – you get “Allow / No” (also over another app); then their clicks are taps, drags are swipes, holding is a long press, Esc = Back, Home = home screen. The first time, turn on “Velivo – remote help” in Settings → Accessibility (Velivo shows where). If Android says “Restricted setting” (Velivo is not from the Play Store): App info → ⋮ → “Allow restricted settings”, then turn it on in Accessibility. Velivo does not read the screen; control ends with 🖥 or when the call ends. Without control, their click = a 👆 hint.
* **🎬 Video messages:** phone – 📎 → 🎬 Record a video message; Windows – the 🎬 button (camera recording, ⏺ start, ⏹ send, up to 3 min). Your own 🎤 and 🎬 recordings stay in Velivo – tap to replay them yourself.
* **↩ Reply, 😀 React, ↪ Forward, ✏ Edit:** long-press a message (Windows: right-click). A reply shows a quote above the text; a reaction (👍❤️😂😮😢🙏) appears next to the time – the same one again removes it; edit your own message within 15 min (“✏” for everyone); forward – text or a file to chosen people and groups.
* **📞 Voice call:** the 📞 button in a chat (no camera, phone at your ear); 📹 – video.
* **🖼 Chat files:** the 🖼 button – all photos, videos, voice messages and files of the chat in one place.
* **👤 My profile:** phone – 👤 on the chat list, Windows – 🎨: profile photo (shown to contacts instead of the dot) and 🔒 messenger lock (phone fingerprint / PIN, Windows Hello). **🔑 Security key** (YubiKey: phone – hold it to NFC or plug into USB and touch; Windows – USB): the messenger opens with the key; add a second, backup key; cancelling the key window = backup: phone fingerprint / PIN, Windows Hello.
* **🔗 My Velivo address (like a WhatsApp number):** ➕ → **🔗 My Velivo address** – a link for anyone with Velivo (📤 Share). They: ➕ → **🔗 I have someone's Velivo address…** → paste → request. You get “👤 … wants to contact you” → **Add / Decline** (also away from home). The new person gets **the messenger only** – not your home network, films or sync. **🔄 Change** – the old link stops working. Needs a relay (NUCBOX) in contacts.
* **A Velivo address in a chat:** a message with someone's address has a **👤 Send a contact request** button – one tap. **🔗 I have someone's address** pastes a copied address by itself.
* **🔗 Link preview (like Signal):** a message with a link shows a card with the site name, page title and description. The sender fetches them and sends them encrypted – the recipient and the relay never contact the site. Tap the card to open the page.
* **🛡 Scam protection in chats:** a link imitating a bank or a known company (e.g. “mbank” on a foreign site, look-alike letters) gets a red warning under the message and a question before opening. A request for a BLIK code, an SMS code, card details or an “extra payment” gets a yellow hint: call and make sure. Checked on the phone, nothing is sent anywhere (also on Windows).
* **♿ Accessibility** (♿ on the chat list): 🔠 big text in chats, 🔊 new messages read aloud, 💡 camera flash and strong vibration on messages and calls (for people hard of hearing – the call flashes while it rings), ◐ high contrast.

On some phones Android does not allow speech recognition during a call – then “Captions unavailable” is shown. Both people need the new Velivo.

The drawings are simplified – **📷 letter** or **📷 word** replaces them with your photo (e.g. of a signing person); a photo of a whole word is shown instead of its letters – your own sign glossary. Photos stay on the phone. Polish letters are shown as base letters (ą → a).

## 9. When something goes wrong

- If Velivo closes because of an error, next time it opens it shows **"Velivo closed because of an error"** with a **Copy** button – send that description to the Velivo developer (no addresses or page content – just how the error happened).
- An advert Velivo doesn't hide: 🎯 Remove element → tap it → **📋** copies the element's code for a report.
- A site misbehaves because of ad blocking: the privacy panel (the 🛡 counter in the menu) → **Turn off blocking here**.
- Reading or music stops after the screen turns off (Xiaomi): Settings → Apps → Velivo → Battery saver → **No restrictions**.
- The wake-up didn't ring: check the phone isn't muted and that Velivo has **Alarms & reminders** permission in Android Settings.

## 10. Installing, updates and app versions

- **Installing from the file** `Velivo-pelna.apk` (e.g. from WhatsApp): the phone asks to allow installs from that source. Google Play Protect warns about **every** app from outside Google Play – choose **More details → Install anyway**. It does not mean Velivo is harmful; there is no warning when installing from Google Play.
- **Updates:** open the new `Velivo-pelna.apk` and tap **Update** – it installs over the old version and everything stays (settings, channel lists, favourites, passwords, notes). **Do not uninstall** the old version – you would lose your data.
- **Three versions from one code base:**

| Version | File | For whom | YouTube |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full | `Velivo-pelna.apk` | You, family, friends (install from the file) | No adverts, in the background, "YouTube · no adverts" bar, My songs |
| Store | `Velivo-sklep.aab` | Google Play – careful | No ad blocking on YouTube and no YouTube in the background |
| Store YT | `Velivo-sklepyt.aab` | Google Play – like Brave | Ad blocking and background play on YouTube too, without advertising it |

Everything else (browser, privacy, TV, films, Jellyfin, notes, sync) is the same. The full version and the store versions are different apps for the phone – they update separately (file / Google Play).
